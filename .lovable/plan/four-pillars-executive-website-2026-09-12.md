# Four Pillars Executive Website

## Goal
Build a polished, single-page corporate website for Four Pillars Business Services that uses the supplied emerald-and-gold identity and presents the Nirvana Residences I partnership as a premium featured opportunity.

## Experience
- Create a refined executive visual system using deep emerald, warm champagne gold, ivory, crisp serif-led display typography, restrained motion, sharp spacing, and subtle material texture.
- Build a sticky branded header with anchored navigation for About, Four Pillars, Services, Sectors, Opportunities, Global Footprint, and Contact.
- Open with a high-impact, image-led introduction using the supplied tagline, supporting copy, and two clear actions.
- Keep the composition responsive and highly legible across desktop and mobile.

## Page Sections
1. **Introduction** — Four Pillars positioning, cross-border remit, and calls to explore opportunities or schedule a consultation.
2. **About** — concise Dubai/Australia-founded company profile and “Building Bridges Across Borders” philosophy.
3. **Four Fundamental Pillars** — Strategy, Market Access, Network, and Execution in a distinctive four-part editorial layout.
4. **Services** — six advisory and growth services with concise outcome-oriented descriptions.
5. **Industry Sectors** — Education, Real Estate, Hospitality, and Food & Consumer Products.
6. **Featured Opportunity** — Meraki Developers and Nirvana Residences I, using selected imagery and verified facts from the supplied presentation and brochure: Me’aisem location, travel times, building description, unit mix, starting prices, and amenities.
7. **Global Footprint** — an interactive regional selector for GCC, South Asia, Africa, and Australia, updating the displayed market narrative without leaving the page.
8. **Consultation** — validated inquiry form covering contact/company details, sector, target region, and message, with a polished confirmation state.
9. **Footer** — corporate identity, philosophy, navigation, and contact prompt.

## Assets and Interactions
- Use the supplied brand image as the visual identity reference; create a clean text/mark treatment suitable for the navigation rather than placing the reference board as a banner.
- Extract and use a curated set of authentic Nirvana renderings/interiors from the supplied brochure and presentation.
- Make the supplied brochure available through a download action.
- Add smooth anchored navigation, mobile menu, regional tabs, form validation, and restrained reveal transitions with reduced-motion support.

## Technical Details
- Implement at `/` with semantic HTML and a single H1.
- Extend the existing Tailwind token system with semantic emerald, gold, ivory, and surface roles; avoid hardcoded page colors.
- Keep all interaction client-side; the consultation form will validate and confirm locally because no submission destination or email service was provided.
- Add complete page metadata for Four Pillars Business Services.
- Verify the finished page in desktop and mobile viewports, including navigation, regional switching, brochure access, form errors/success, layout overflow, and browser console health.
