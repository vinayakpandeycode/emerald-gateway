# Emerald Gateway

Build an executive, luxury corporate website for Four Pillars Business Services, a Dubai-based cross-border consulting and business development firm.

Design & Aesthetic:
- Match the brand identity from the attached logo image (uploads/5c6c8660-5cb2-4cb8-874e-10f18804f451): deep emerald green, champagne/warm gold accents, crisp typography, and refined modern executive styling.

Key Sections & Content:
1. Header / Navigation: Logo branding, links to About, The Four Pillars, Services, Sectors, Featured Opportunities, Global Footprint, and Contact.
2. Hero Section:
   - Tagline: "Connecting Markets. Creating Opportunities. Scaling Businesses."
   - Subtitle: "Dubai-based cross-border consulting and business development helping companies, investors, and institutions unlock international growth across South Asia, Africa, the GCC, and Australia."
   - Prominent CTAs: "Explore Opportunities" and "Schedule a Consultation".
3. The Four Fundamental Pillars:
   - Strategy: Understanding direction and architecting the roadmap.
   - Market Access: Identifying high-potential markets and entry pathways.
   - Network: Connecting with key decision-makers, operators, and capital.
   - Execution: Turning strategic opportunities into commercial outcomes.
4. Services Suite:
   - Strategic Consulting
   - Cross-Border Market Entry
   - Strategic Partnerships
   - Business Expansion & Scaling
   - Opportunity & Investment Advisory
   - Network & Market Access
5. Industry Sectors:
   - Education, Real Estate, Hospitality, Food & Consumer Products.
6. Featured Real Estate Opportunity (Strategic Partner Showcase):
   - Highlight the Meraki Developers partnership and Nirvana Residences I (from the attached presentation and brochure).
   - Display project highlights: Me'aisem location, connectivity (Dubai Hills Mall, Airport, Palm Jumeirah), building specs (2B+G+2P+19F+Rooftop, 127 studios, 178 1BR, 84 2BR), starting prices (~AED 781K / A$297K), and residential amenities.
   - Include a brochure download / project inquiry CTA.
7. Global Footprint:
   - Interactive regional showcase covering GCC, South Asia, Africa, and Australia.
8. Consultation / Contact Form:
   - Inquiries by sector, target region, and company details with clean validation.
9. Footer: Complete corporate identity, philosophy ("Building Bridges Across Borders"), and quick links.
